Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

QuotePro is an internal estimate-pricing tool for Grand Valley Garage Doors employees. It is not a customer-facing service: customers never create a QuotePro account and never enter information into QuotePro directly.

What we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in, we store your email address and a password hash through Supabase Auth. We never see your password.
  • Estimate data. Door, window, and miscellaneous dealer costs, pricing variables, and calculated prices that you enter, paste, or scan from a quote document (OCR). Saved quotes are stored in our database so you can reopen, restore, and delete them from History.
  • API keys. Keys you create for the pricing API are stored as hashes only. The plaintext key is shown once, when it is created, and never again.
  • On your device. A copy of your recent estimate history is kept in your browser's local storage, and the Tampermonkey userscript keeps its API key in Tampermonkey storage on your own machine. Both stay on your device unless you clear them.

How we use it

We use this information only to run QuotePro: to sign you in, to calculate estimate prices, and to save and retrieve your quotes. We do not show ads, we do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Third-party services

QuotePro runs on Supabase, which hosts our authentication and database. The Tampermonkey userscript runs in your browser on the iStore and ServiceTitan pages you are already using for work; it reads dealer costs from the iStore page and sends them to QuotePro's own pricing API (/v1/calculate) to get prices back. No other third party receives your estimate data.

Retention and deletion

Saved quotes are kept until you delete them from History in the app. Your browser-local history is removed when you clear your browser storage. To delete your account, contact your Grand Valley Garage Doors administrator.

Questions

Questions about this policy? Contact your Grand Valley Garage Doors administrator.
